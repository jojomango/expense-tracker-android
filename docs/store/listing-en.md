## App name

Expense Tracker

## Short description (77 chars)

Private, offline budget tracker. No sign-up, no ads. See what's left this week.

## Full description

"How much can I still spend this week?" Open the app and the answer is right at the top.

Your data stays on your phone
No account, no sign-in, no ads. Every entry is stored only on your own phone and is never uploaded anywhere. (Settings has an optional "Help improve the app" switch, off by default. If you turn it on, only anonymous usage and crash info is sent — never your entries.)

Weekly budget at a glance
Set a weekly budget and the home screen shows what's left this week, how many days remain and how much you can spend per day. Overspending is clearly marked. For trips, switch to a total budget to see what's left for the whole journey.

Multiple wallets and currencies
Everyday spending, a trip to Japan, household costs — keep them apart. Each wallet has its own currency and they never get mixed up: yen stays yen, dollars stay dollars.

Add an entry in seconds
A custom number pad and category icons make logging fast. Pick today, yesterday or any date, and add a note.

See where your money goes
The stats screen shows this week's or this month's spending by category in a donut chart, plus an 8-week spending trend.

Your own categories
Common categories like Food, Transport and Housing are built in, and you can add your own with custom icons and colors.

You own your backups
Export a backup file with one tap and save it wherever you like (for example, your cloud drive). Import it on a new phone to restore everything. The app reminds you to back up every 7 days.

The details matter
Dark mode, a configurable first day of the week, TalkBack screen reader support, and both English and Traditional Chinese.

Like it? Buy the developer a coffee
The app is free and every feature is free. If it helps you, you can support the developer through Google Play under "Support the developer" in Settings.
